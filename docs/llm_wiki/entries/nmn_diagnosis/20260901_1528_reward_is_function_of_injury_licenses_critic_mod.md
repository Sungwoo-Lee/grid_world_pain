# The reward IS a function of injury — critic modulation is licensed

## Key conclusion
An earlier claim in this project's own FiLM/RL discussion log — that our reward is not a
function of injury, and therefore PAPL's stated precondition for modulating the critic is
unmet — is **false**. Injury is literally an argument of the reward. The precondition is met,
and more strongly than an additive injury term would give.

## Evidence, measurements, facts
- `src/environment/core.py:49-53` — `calculate_drive(satiation, injury, params)` returns the
  L2 distance of `(satiation, injury)` from `(setpoint, 0)`.
- `core.py:728-731` — `reward_homeostatic = prev_drive - curr_drive` under
  `use_homeostatic_reward`; healing lowers the drive and is rewarded directly.
- `configs/environment/default.yaml:181` sets `use_homeostatic_reward: true`; verified live
  for `basic/04` and `sensory_directional/A_baseline`.
- The coupling is non-separable: dD/d(injury) = injury/D, so the marginal value of healing
  rises with injury AND depends on satiation through D. This is the "managing conflicting
  needs" case, not a simple additive term.
- **Footgun found while verifying**: two different "drive" quantities live in the same
  function. `calculate_drive` (unnormalised L2) feeds the reward; `drive_hunger` /
  `drive_injury` (`core.py:725-726`, normalised sum of squares) are logging-only — and the
  inline comment describing the latter sits directly above the reward computation that uses
  the former. Anyone reading that block to understand the reward gets the wrong function.

## Decisions and actions
- Corrected at source in the ideas doc §6.2 (commit `c36a6355`); the stale open-question
  phrasing swept in `e9135102`.
- The error had already propagated into `MODULATION_SITE_REFACTOR.md`; plan-reviewer raised
  it as its one Critical, and the plan was amended (`2e7da8b5`) to assert the licence rather
  than disclaim it.
- Critic modulation is therefore a supported arm of the coming 2-factor experiment.

## Open questions and follow-ups
- Residual caveats that DO survive: PAPL's conditioner is an open-loop clock while ours is
  contingent sensed injury arriving through an alpha-kernel delay; and Marquis shows the sign
  of critic conditioning is mechanism-dependent (positive for FiLM, negative for LoRA).
