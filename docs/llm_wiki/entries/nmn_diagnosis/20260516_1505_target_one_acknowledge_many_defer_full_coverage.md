# Target-one-acknowledge-many-defer-full-coverage scoping rule

## Key conclusion

User clarified mid-symposium that the project's "no-one-to-one neuromodulator-function mapping" constraint does NOT mean "no channel attribution at all". The refined rule: **targeting a specific neuromodulator (Doya-style) is valid and necessary for a single paper — without a target the paper is uninterpretable. But full coverage of all neuromodulator-function mappings by one-to-one is mathematically impossible because biological functions are overlapped and distributed.** The middle path: target one system → produce focused result → acknowledge the targeted modulator produces multiple downstream effects (one-to-many) → defer full coverage to future work.

## Evidence, measurements, facts

- User's verbatim clarification: "We CAN design a study plan to observe Doya and previous studies' suggested target neuromodulatory functions as a single paper. But this CANNOT be done fully as the functions are overlapped and distributed. If we suggest no target, the paper is uninterpretable; if we aim for full coverage by one-to-one, that's mathematically impossible."
- The original constraint ("no one-to-one") given to the four symposium professors was the maximal reading. Three of four professor memos over-generalised to "no channel attribution at all"; the postdoc applied the refined version when synthesising.
- Lee 2024 §6 is the published precedent for partial neuromodulator coverage — Lee explicitly drops the 5-HT branch ("no convergent normative theory") rather than half-claim it. The project follows the same decision pattern: target NA (Aston-Jones & Cohen 2005 LC signature matches the R2 anchor); defer ACh + DA + 5-HT to future work.
- v5 of the direction memo embeds this in `targeted_neuromodulator: NA (noradrenergic / locus coeruleus)` frontmatter and explicitly names ACh + DA + 5-HT as out-of-scope under §5.9.

## Decisions and actions

- v5 of the direction memo absorbed the refined rule: §1 + §3 name NA/LC explicitly; §5.9 adds "full coverage of ACh + DA + 5-HT systems" as a substrate-NOT entry.
- Postdoc synthesis applied the refined constraint when collecting the four professor contributions, correcting any over-generalisation.
- This rule generalises beyond the NMN project: any project with incomplete biological-system coverage should target one explicitly + defer the rest.
