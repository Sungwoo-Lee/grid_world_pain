# Nutrition runway + wander-policy confounds the hg10 "hypervigilance" read

## Key conclusion
A nutrition sweep on hg10 (injury fixed at 0, nutrition swept 10/30/50/70/100, on the 7×7 conflict-probe geometry) shows the agent's "eat-failure" is driven primarily by its **initial nutrition** and a **wander-away-from-adjacent-food policy**, NOT by avoidance of the animal. The agent does not eat the food it starts next to — it executes a learned exploration policy that wanders off; the early trajectory is **identical across nutrition levels** (nutrition only sets the survival runway). At very low nutrition it **starves even with NO animal present**. This **confounds the earlier "unconditional hypervigilance" reading** [[20260622_1744_hypervig_probe_hg10_overgeneralizes_threat]], whose high-injury/low-nutrition start (injury 70, nutrition 20) sat right at the starvation-runway floor. The animal is a **secondary modulator** — it raises the nutrition threshold needed to survive — not the cause of the food-avoidance.

## Evidence, measurements, facts
- Eat-vs-flee map (injury 0; EAT/501 = ate & survived full, FLEE/NN = never reached food & starved at NN, ate/NN = reached food but predator killed it):
  - **no-animal**:      nutr10 FLEE/11 | nutr30+ EAT/501
  - **wander rabbit**:  nutr10 FLEE/11 | nutr30+ EAT/501  (identical to no-animal — benign rabbit has ~no effect)
  - **chasing rabbit**: nutr10 FLEE/11 | nutr30 FLEE/31 | nutr50+ EAT/501
  - **predator**:       nutr10 FLEE/11 | nutr30 ate/41 | nutr50 ate/111 | nutr70 ate/141 | nutr100 EAT/501
- Step dump (no-animal): at BOTH nutr10 and nutr30 the agent starts at food-dist 1 and walks AWAY (dist 1→2→…→7), nutrition strictly decreasing (no eating). nutr10 dies step 11; nutr30 has runway to forage later → EAT/501. Same early positions → policy is nutrition-independent; nutrition = runway only.
- Configs: configs/environment/experiment/behavior_probes/explore/nutrition_sweep/ (20 configs). Model: hg10 step 7200007. Canonical renderer.py eval.
- Probe caveat: the agent wandering off an *adjacent* food suggests the 7×7 single-corner-food geometry is **off-distribution** vs hg10's 10×10 quadrant-food training.
