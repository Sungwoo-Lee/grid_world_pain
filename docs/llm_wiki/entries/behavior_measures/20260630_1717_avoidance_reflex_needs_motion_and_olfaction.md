# Avoidance: flee-to-cover needs approach motion AND recognizable olfaction; injury is not preemptive

## Key conclusion
In the avoidance probe (agent centre, `hides_agent` bush, one animal approaching), the agent dives into the bush as cover — but the flee-to-cover reflex is gated by **two necessary conditions together: (1) an animal approaching/chasing to adjacency, AND (2) a recognizable olfactory signature**. A *wandering* animal (no approach) is ignored like empty space; a *chasing* animal whose predator-identifiable smell channel is **zeroed** is ALSO ignored (the agent lets it walk onto its cell). Threat-vs-harmless *discrimination* appears only in the *sustained* phase and is **damage-driven**. **Injury does NOT make avoidance earlier or more preemptive** — the flee trigger is identical at injury 0 and 70.

## Evidence, measurements, facts
- Subject: in-distribution model (ckpt 8900007). 2×3 matrix {predator, chasing-rabbit, none} × injury{0,70}, plus wander-rabbit and smell-ablation variants; single-seed videos + 30-seed statistics (small initial-state jitter).
- Reflex (recognizable chaser, predator OR harmless chasing-rabbit): flees at animal distance 1, reaches bush ~step 6 — flee-rate 1.00±0.00, identical at both injury levels. Predator-vs-rabbit *identity* does not change the reflex.
- WANDER rabbit (no chase) → entered-bush ~0%, behaves like the no-animal control. → approach motion is necessary.
- Olfactory ablation (CHASING rabbit, smell `[0,0.7,0.5]`→`[0,0,0.5]`, predator-identifiable channel zeroed; visual unchanged) → entered-bush 0%, agent gets caught. → recognizable olfaction is necessary. **This corrects the earlier too-strong "avoidance is purely motion-triggered" claim made the same day.** Predator and rabbit smells both trigger; only a degraded smell abolishes it. Hypothesis: olfactory channel 1 (high in BOTH predator 0.7 and neutral 0.5) is an "animal-present" signal; channel 2 is the discriminator.
- Discrimination (sustained): agent keeps evading the predator (takes hits, Δinjury +22±25 at inj0) but TOLERATES the harmless chasing rabbit adjacent (proximity 0.46±0.09 vs predator 0.34±0.08, ~7 SE apart).
- Injury heals over time when not hit (start 70 → ~0; negative Δinjury). High injury alone never triggers hiding (control 0% bush at inj70).

## Decisions and actions
- Promoted the `avoidance` probe set (12 configs) to `core/`.
- Validated avoidance measures: bush-use rate, steps-to-reach-bush, time-in-bush, flight-initiation distance (FID), time-near-animal, closest-approach, injury-change, survival.

## Open questions and follow-ups
- Channel-role hypothesis (ch1 = animal-present, ch2 = discriminator) untested — zero ch2 instead, or keep ch1 / drop ch2, and re-run.
- Bush-distance sweep would make FID and steps-to-reach-bush non-degenerate (graded measures).
- Single-seed for some ablations; multi-seed confirmation pending.
