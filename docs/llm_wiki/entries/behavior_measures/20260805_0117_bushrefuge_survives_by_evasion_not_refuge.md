# Bush-refuge agents survive by evasion, not by using the refuge

> **⚠️ CORRECTED / SUPERSEDED (2026-08-10).** This insight is WRONG: it read the
> `bush_dwell` column as *steps* and concluded the agents 'don't hide' (~0). In fact
> `bush_dwell` is a FRACTION (0-1)=%, so those values are 34-42% (predator) etc. — the
> agents DO hide heavily. See the corrected insight [[20260810_1753_bushrefuge_injury_suppresses_bush_use_heal_by_rest]].

## Key conclusion
The four `rppo_bushrefuge_b0{1..4}` agents were trained with the bush as a PHYSICAL refuge (`obstacles[bush].blocks_animals: true` — predators/rabbit cannot enter, agent still hides). Probed with a regime-matched battery, they survive predator encounters well (b01/b02 ride to the full 501-step cap in every predator episode; b04 reaches ~46 survival on the hard predator+injury probe vs Dreamer's ~9) — but they do it by EVADING in the open, NOT by sitting in the bush. `bush_dwell` in predator conditions is effectively zero and stays there across the whole training history.

## Evidence, measurements, facts
- `bush_dwell` (steps in bush, out of a 100-step probe episode), mean +/- std across ALL checkpoints, predator conditions:
  - avoid_pred_inj00: b01 0.14+/-0.24 (n=119), b02 0.04+/-0.12 (n=102), b03 0.42+/-0.43 (n=233), b04 0.34+/-0.42 (n=253).
  - avoid_pred_inj70: b01 0.13+/-0.25, b02 0.08+/-0.12, b03 0.33+/-0.41, b04 0.35+/-0.43.
  - **0.0% of checkpoints have bush_dwell > 5 steps; max ever ~0.94-0.96 steps.** So this is NOT a metastable-flicker artifact (cf. [[20260710_1635_bush_hiding_metastable_dwell_measure]]) — it is consistent across 100-253 checkpoints per agent.
- Calibration: the non-bushrefuge `rppo_basic03_randinit` agent dwelt 46-57 steps when in its hiding mode (heatmap b03_randinit_8p5M_hiding). Bushrefuge agents are ~2 orders of magnitude lower.
- Trained-env (own bushrefuge env, cap 501): b01 & b02 survive to 501 in 30/30 predator episodes; b03/b04 mixed (72-501).
- rPPO massively outsurvives the Dreamer arms on the same probe family (501 / 46 vs Dreamer bins6/hier/curriculum ~9-30).
- Data: `results/eval/avoidance/bushrefuge/rppo/<label>/*.csv`, videos under `videos_probe/` + `videos_trainenv/`.

## Decisions and actions
- Reported the near-zero-dwell finding to the user as an OPEN QUESTION (evasion vs refuge), not a "refuge works" conclusion; trained-env videos are the screen to confirm the kiting pattern.
- Methodological lesson recorded: a single-checkpoint `@last` dwell read is noisy; characterise a measure by mean+/-std (and %-of-checkpoints-above-threshold) across the checkpoint history.
- Snapshot of STILL-TRAINING runs (10-25M steps) — re-runnable incrementally.

## Open questions and follow-ups
- Do the agents ever learn to USE the refuge later in training, or is open-field evasion a stable terminal strategy? (Consistent with [[20260630_1718_cover_use_late_emerging_run_vs_hide]]: early models RUN/kite rather than dive into cover.)
- Why does a guaranteed-safe refuge go unused — is evasion simply easier to learn on these grid sizes / predator speeds?
