# NMN as scaling-and-shifting implementation of RL-hyperparameter modulation (v4)

## 1. What this direction proposes

This memo redirects the project's neuromodulator-network work away from "the **NMN** (a small side-network that watches the agent's internal state and emits two signals — a multiplicative scale $\gamma$ and an additive shift $\beta$ — into the main policy) helps **continual learning**" toward a stronger but **honestly narrower** claim: **the NMN's algorithm — multiplicatively scale and additively shift the policy's internal activations — is simultaneously the cellular-level account of biological neuromodulation (gain control on target neurons) and the behaviour-level account of how *two* of Doya 2002's four RL-hyperparameter channels get modulated state-by-state**. The bridge is a **FiLM** layer (feature-wise linear modulation: $h \mapsto \gamma(c) \odot h + \beta(c)$). Our anchor — the modulator beat the unmodulated baseline by approximately 25× the seed-noise floor on the second return to a previously-seen task stage ([memory insight 20260513_0014](../../../.claude-memory/memories/nmn_diagnosis/20260513_0014_nmn_r2_continual_h1b_h1c_confirmed_high_margin.md)) — is reread as a readout of a state-dependent hyperparameter shift, not as continual-learning resistance.

**What v4 narrows from v3.** v3 claimed four channels carried by the substrate (ACh-as-α at encoder; "effective discount on past memory" at the GRU update gate; NA-as-β at policy logits; 5-HT-as-γ as a weak fourth). v4 narrows to **two cleanly carried channels** — ACh-as-α at the encoder (site A) and NA-as-β at the policy logits (site C) — and re-names the GRU-update-gate site (site B) as **plasticity gating** rather than effective-discount, after the math-reviewer audit of the underlying concept memo ([film_neuromod_integration.md §10 flag (d)](../concepts/film_neuromod_integration.md)) ruled the "additive $\beta$ at GRU update gate $\equiv$ effective Bellman $\gamma$" claim dimensionally incoherent. The 5-HT / Bellman-discount channel (Doya 2002 §3.2) is honestly named as **out-of-substrate** in §5.9 — a forward-pass FiLM operator cannot produce a quantity whose mathematical content is "scalar inside a temporal sum"; that channel lives at the loss / TD-target layer, not in the forward pass. Lee 2024 §6 made the same call and explicitly dropped 5-HT for the same reason. v4 follows Lee 2024 on this point.

**Why narrowing strengthens the memo.** The unification claim is now tighter, fully lineage-grounded (see §3), and survives a careful reviewer who would otherwise correctly flag the v3 four-channel claim. The empirical critical path (the four-arm γ/β clamp at site C, the per-site dissociation matrix, the T/P split) is **unchanged** — those experiments were always organised around α and β, not γ_Bellman.

## 2. The two lineages

DA ↔ learning rate / TD error; **ACh ↔ learning rate $\alpha$** (Doya 2002 §3.4 — chosen over the out-of-corpus Yu & Dayan precision branch); NA ↔ inverse temperature; 5-HT ↔ discount. | Doya 2002; Lee 2024; Ben-Iwhiwhu 2022; Wang 2024. |
| **Neural gain — scale and shift** | Multiplicative arm scales weights or slope. Additive arm shifts the operating point (rheobase — the current at which a neuron starts firing). | Multiplicative: Tsuda 2021 (weight scaling; hypertube is a *consequence*), Costacurta 2024, Rodriguez-Garcia 2026. v1 mis-cited Tsuda 2021 as additive; it is multiplicative-on-weights. Ferguson & Cardin Box 1c is the additive anchor.

## 3. The unification claim

A single FiLM layer on the policy's activations carries **two of Doya's four channels** cleanly and **proxies — does not implement — a third**. The substrate's three injection sites (A encoder, B GRU update gate, C policy logits) instantiate this as:

- **Site A (encoder pre-fusion) — α channel.** ACh-as-learning-rate (Doya 2002 §3.4; Lee 2024 §3.1). Per-feature gain on the encoder's pre-fusion activations is the activation-level analogue of a per-feature learning-rate adjustment on the representation.
- **Site C (policy logits) — β channel.** NA-as-inverse-temperature (Doya 2002 §3.3; Lee 2024 §3.4 Eq. 4). Scaling actor logits is, on the multiplicative arm, a direct softmax-temperature change. The additive arm shifts the action-prior independently.
- **Site B (GRU update gate) — plasticity gating, NOT γ_Bellman.** Additive $\beta^{(B)}$ shifts the per-unit "stay vs update" decision boundary, modulating per-unit hidden-state retention (slow vs fast effective memory). This is plasticity gating — a per-unit retention quantity bounded by $(0,1)$ — not the Bellman value-function discount, which is a global scalar inside a temporal sum (see [film_neuromod_integration.md §10 flag (d)](../concepts/film_neuromod_integration.md) and [film_hypernet_bnn_lineage_and_critic_modulation.md §3.1](../concepts/film_hypernet_bnn_lineage_and_critic_modulation.md)).

The **fourth Doya channel (5-HT, γ_Bellman)** is **out of the forward-pass FiLM substrate**. Lee 2024 §6 made this call and dropped the 5-HT branch; v4 follows. The honest naming sits in §5.9 below.

**Scalar-to-population bridge.** `mod_h` is the Doya-side scalar — one nucleus, one tone; the FiLM read-out matrix is the receptor-density-analog fan-out. Per-feature $\gamma, \beta \in \mathbb{R}^d$ is the population dimension biology already commits to.

**Level-specific precision reading (added in v3 from `professor-bayesian-brain`, preserved in v4).** The same three injection sites map onto three precision levels of a hierarchical predictive-coding generative model: **A (encoder) ↔ sensory precision $\Pi_s$ (how much to trust the observation); B (GRU update gate) ↔ state-transition precision $\Pi_z$ (how much to trust the prior over hidden states); C (policy logits) ↔ policy precision $\Pi_\pi$ (how sharply to commit to one policy)**. This reading does not displace the Doya-channel reading — it is the same architecture seen through the active-inference lens. The two readings make the same predictions when the modulator is doing one-knob work and dissociating predictions when the architecture is doing genuine multi-channel work; we operationalise both below.

### §3.0 Family-tree placement of the substrate (lineage callout)

The new investigation memo on FiLM ⊂ Hypernet ⊂ Bayesian Hypernet vs BNN ([film_hypernet_bnn_lineage_and_critic_modulation.md §2](../concepts/film_hypernet_bnn_lineage_and_critic_modulation.md)) makes five formal lineage claims that v4 absorbs as the architectural ancestry of the project's substrate. In compact form:

1. **FiLM ⊂ Hypernet (rigorous).** A FiLM layer (per-channel affine on activations) is a hypernetwork whose generated "weights" are restricted to per-channel diagonal scaling plus a constant translation. Independent routes through Ha 2016 §3.2 (the scaling-vector trick that produces FiLM at per-row granularity two years before FiLM was named) and Abdollahzadeh 2021 (FiLM ≡ per-output-channel-uniform kernel modulation; KML is the general per-weight case). The project's three-site FiLM substrate **is** a hypernetwork; the modulator is its generator $\phi$.
2. **Hypernet ⊆ point-mass BHN (rigorous, as a *limit*).** A deterministic hypernetwork is the limiting case of a Bayesian Hypernetwork (BHN — Krueger 2017) whose implicit posterior over weights collapses to a point mass: $q_\phi(W \mid c) = \delta(W - g_\phi(c))$. This is a limiting inclusion, not a sub-architecture obtained by output-space restriction — calling a deterministic hypernet "a BHN" without the "as a limit" qualifier would over-state the lineage.
3. **BNN ⊥ BHN (rigorous, orthogonal).** A Bayesian Neural Network (BNN — Kendall & Gal 2017 in the corpus) places a posterior on the *main network's leaf weights* without context-conditioning. A Bayesian Hypernetwork places a posterior on the *generator*, inducing context-conditional weight distributions. The two architectures place uncertainty at different levels and are not nested. They can in principle combine; no paper in either of the project's corpora does so.
4. **FiLM-Ensemble ≈ finite-mixture BHN approximation (holds with caveat).** Turkoglu 2022's FiLM-Ensemble — M deterministic settings of $(\gamma^m, \beta^m)$ averaged at predict time — has predictive distribution equal to a Monte Carlo estimate of $\mathbb{E}_q[\sigma(f_{\theta,\gamma,\beta}(x))]$ under a discrete categorical posterior, with within-member / between-member variance decomposition matching Kendall & Gal 2017 Eq. 7. The caveat: members are trained jointly under one objective rather than sampled from a learned posterior; there is no KL-to-prior; the gain-init heuristic controls spread. Honest framing: **finite-mixture approximation of a posterior over FiLM scalars**, not a clean BHN.
5. **Galanti & Wolf 2020 Thm 4 modularity carries through (rigorous).** The hypernetwork modularity bound — $N_g = O(\epsilon^{-m_1/r})$ parameters for a hypernet to approximate any target in $W^{r,m}$ to error $\epsilon$, vs $\Omega(\epsilon^{-(m_1 + m_2)/r})$ for embedding-concatenation — applies to every member of the family. FiLM, deterministic-Hypernet, BHN, and FiLM-Ensemble inherit the same parameter-efficient capacity allocation.

**Where the project's substrate sits.** Today: deterministic, context-conditional (the top-right cell of the 2×2 over {deterministic, stochastic} × {unconditional, context-conditional}). By Claim 1 the substrate is a hypernetwork; by Claim 2 it is the point-mass-BHN limit; by Claim 3 it is orthogonal to BNN (not nested under it); by Claim 5 it inherits hypernetwork modularity. The compound formula in [film_neuromod_integration.md §5.4 Row EE-6](../concepts/film_neuromod_integration.md) — FiLM-Ensemble + Kendall-Gal heteroscedastic precision on the modulator's output — is the architectural upgrade that moves the substrate one row down in the 2×2 toward stochastic context-conditional weights. By Claim 4 this compound is **the first instantiation of an approximate Bayesian Hypernetwork with a heteroscedastic likelihood for an RL policy**: cleanly positioned against Krueger 2017 (full BHN; no heteroscedastic head; not RL) and Kendall & Gal 2017 (BNN; no context-conditioning; not RL). The novelty of EE-6 survives the lineage; what it gains is honest ancestry.

**Differentiation from Rodriguez-Garcia 2026.** Rodriguez-Garcia modulates the **gradient** (optimiser-level — gain reparameterises learning rate as $\lambda \to \lambda / g^2$); this project modulates the **forward activation** (PPO's Adam untouched). Both invoke noradrenergic gain at orthogonal points; the two could combine later. v4 makes the parallel sharper: Rodriguez-Garcia leaves the forward-pass substrate for a *gradient-level α channel*; v4 (Option A in [investigation memo §3.5](../concepts/film_hypernet_bnn_lineage_and_critic_modulation.md)) does not leave the substrate at all — it accepts that the substrate cannot carry the 5-HT/γ_Bellman channel and acknowledges that boundary instead of architecting around it. A v5 follow-up could leave the substrate for a learned-discount head feeding the TD target (Candidate b/d in the investigation memo), at which point the structural parallel to Rodriguez-Garcia becomes load-bearing: both architectures keep the forward-pass FiLM in place and bolt a second mechanism onto the loss / gradient. v4 does not commit to this.

**What `mod_h` is likely doing today.** Under the single-recurrent-state design, `mod_h` is most likely a Noradrenaline / inverse-temperature signal with two passenger heads.

**Prerequisite — the T/P split.** The unification is non-identifiable without the **Phase 0 T/P split** (two recurrent states with separable phasic and tonic timescales). A single `mod_h` forces correlated movement across knobs biology dissociates. This memo escalates the T/P split from open question to prerequisite.

## 4. What this redirects the project away from

The continual-learning win is one behavioural readout of a hyperparameter shift, not evidence that NMN helps continual learning per se. The closest published precedent is **Lee et al. 2024 §3.1 + Figure 1**, which proposes ACh and NA as modulators of RL hyperparameters in a non-stationary bandit with hand-coded functional forms — and which **explicitly drops the 5-HT/γ branch in §6** because no convergent normative theory exists. The project's contribution is **Lee's two-channel framework extended to a multi-site FiLM substrate, with the mapping emerging from optimisation**, in sequential RL (PPO) rather than bandits, with the FiLM ⊂ Hypernet ⊂ point-mass-BHN-limit lineage made explicit (§3.0) so the substrate's architectural ancestry is no longer implicit.

### 5.1 Headline falsifier — combined γ/β-freeze + |δ|-tracking with three outcome scenarios (β channel at site C)

**Plain-English summary.** At the policy site (site C, where the modulator's signals control how sharply the agent commits to an action), we run a four-arm experiment: full modulator, frozen multiplicative arm, frozen additive arm, both frozen. We then ask which arm carried the load, and whether the surviving arm tracks how surprised the value-head is on each step. Three different outcomes correspond to three different stories about what the modulator is actually doing.

**The four arms (drawn from `professor-rl-bayesian-dl` P-4, `professor-neuromodulation` P3, `professor-bayesian-brain` P4).**

1. *Baseline* — full FiLM, both $\gamma$ and $\beta$ free.
2. *γ-clamp at C* — $\gamma^{(C)} = 1$ throughout; only $\beta^{(C)}$ moves.
3. *β-clamp at C* — $\beta^{(C)} = 0$ throughout; only $\gamma^{(C)}$ moves.
4. *Full clamp at C* — both arms frozen.

**Per-arm measures.** (i) Survival-step recovery half-life after a stage switch; (ii) actor-entropy peri-switch spike amplitude (`train/entropy_loss`); (iii) Pearson correlation of the surviving FiLM-arm output with $|\delta|$ over the recovery window (`r_filmarm_vs_abs_delta_per_clamp` — `professor-rl-bayesian-dl` P-4); (iv) **sensitivity $d'$ and decision criterion under a forced-choice probe trial** (`professor-bayesian-brain` P4 — added in v3; requires the forced-choice probe described in §5.8 to be feasible; if not, demote to future work).

**The three outcomes.**

- **Outcome U (Unification holds at C).** $\gamma$-clamp is more damaging than $\beta$-clamp on survival-step recovery AND entropy spike; the surviving $\beta$-arm correlates with $|\delta|$. Reading: $\gamma^{(C)}$ is doing the inverse-temperature work (Doya-NA-$\beta$ via Lee 2024 Eq. 4, $\beta^{-1}(s) = 1/\langle E(s,\hat a)\rangle$), and the additive arm carries the TD-error-driven shift. The unification claim holds at C. **Under the `professor-bayesian-brain` sharpening**, this outcome is *further refined*: if $\gamma$-clamp degrades $d'$ specifically (not just criterion), the multiplicative arm is implementing **policy precision $\Pi_\pi$** in the predictive-coding sense, not pure inverse-temperature in the Doya-NA-only sense. If $\gamma$-clamp leaves $d'$ untouched and only shifts criterion, the Doya-NA-only reading holds. Both refinements are within the "Unification holds" family — they distinguish a richer Bayesian-brain reading from the bare Doya reading inside the same falsifier arm.
- **Outcome V (Vecoven context-conditioning, no Doya channel).** Both clamps are roughly equally damaging AND neither surviving arm tracks $|\delta|$. Reading: the modulator is a *task-identity detector* (Vecoven 2020 §4.4–4.5 freeze-$z$ precedent), not a Doya-channel modulator. The project's contribution shrinks to "FiLM works on PPO".
- **Outcome T (Tsuda hypertube dynamics dominate).** $\beta$-clamp is more damaging than $\gamma$-clamp. Reading: the additive arm is producing activity-space displacements (Tsuda 2021 Fig. 3 hypertubes — although Tsuda's *mechanism* is multiplicative-on-weights, the project's additive-on-activations is an analogy at the level of the geometric signature, not the mechanism), and the multiplicative arm is not load-bearing. Gain control is the right story; hyperparameter modulation should be walked back.

**Why this is the headline.** The synthesis (`neuromodulatory_algorithms_predictions_synthesis.md` §3.6 / §5.1) flags as load-bearing that **no paper in the 18-paper corpus runs the symmetric two-direction unification test on the same agent on the same trials**. This is the closest the project can get to that test on a forward-pass FiLM substrate — Outcome U is the publishable headline, Outcomes V and T are honest publishable refutations.

**A fifth no-go outcome.** A four-way tie (all four clamps perform the same) refutes the FiLM machinery as load-bearing at all and is a pre-flight check on the modulator's training signal.

### 5.2 α-channel predictions (site A, encoder pre-fusion)

The α channel is **ACh-as-learning-rate** at the encoder. Per-feature gain on the encoder's pre-fusion activations is the activation-level analogue of a per-feature learning-rate adjustment on the representation.

- *Cellular mechanism.* Mixed multiplicative and additive arm (Ferguson & Cardin 2020 Box 1 panels b and c). Perceptual gain on evidence is canonically a mixture (Shine 2021 Fig. 1). `professor-neuromodulation` P7 sharpens this: the multiplicative arm at A should show *divisive normalisation* signature — high $\gamma_{A,i}$ on one feature reduces relative weight of competing features in the downstream policy decision. This is testable as `eval/gamma_A_divisive_index`.
- *RL hyperparameter.* Doya-$\alpha$ (effective learning rate on representation; Doya 2002 §3.4; Lee 2024 §3.1). The encoder's per-feature gain scales how much each input dimension contributes to downstream gradient flow.
- *Bayesian-brain precision level.* **Sensory precision $\Pi_s$** — how much to trust the observation. Adams, Shipp & Friston 2013 hierarchical-precision message-passing; Shine 2021 Box 3.
- *Pain construct.* **Channel-selective hypervigilance** (`professor-pain-modeling` P-Pain-1). Post-injury site-A gain should rise on threat-relevant channels ($\Delta\gamma_{\mathcal{T}\setminus\mathcal{N}} \ge 0.2$ across $\ge 5$ seeds) more than on threat-irrelevant channels, *time-locked to the interoceptive injury signal (lag $\le \min(5, 2\tau_{\text{mod}})$ steps), not to the threat input itself*. Without this dissociation the modulator is doing volume control, not hypervigilance (FP-1 in `pain_vs_nociception_construct.md` §2.3).
- *Project-testbed measure.* (i) `delta_survival_clamp_A` largest on noisy-observation tasks (`professor-rl-bayesian-dl` P-3); (ii) per-modality $\gamma_A$ statistics in event-locked window across the 9 input modalities (`professor-pain-modeling` P-Pain-1); (iii) site-A response decoupling under heteroscedastic noise — large under high-obs-noise / clean-reward, small under clean-obs / stochastic-reward (`professor-bayesian-brain` P1); (iv) `eval/gamma_A_divisive_index` < 1 for divisive-normalisation signature (`professor-neuromodulation` P7).
- *Falsification signature.* If site-A clamp damages all task axes equally, or if $\gamma_A$ rises uniformly across modalities post-injury, the site-A reading collapses to volume control; the joint "ACh-as-learning-rate / sensory-precision / hypervigilance" reading is refuted at A and the modulator becomes a single-channel signal with passenger heads at A.

### 5.3 β-channel predictions (site C, policy logits)

The β channel is **NA-as-inverse-temperature** at the policy logits. Scaling actor logits is, on the multiplicative arm, a direct softmax-temperature change.

- *Cellular mechanism.* Multiplicative arm dominant ($\gamma_C$) — Ferguson & Cardin 2020 Box 1b. Scaling actor logits *is* a slope change on the softmax (`professor-neuromodulation` P3 — multiplicative is the only operation that produces a temperature movement). The additive arm $\beta_C$ shifts the decision boundary between actions and is the rheobase-on-policy analogue. Per math-reviewer audit minor revision (5) on the concept memo §5.4 Row EE-1: $\gamma_C \equiv 1/T$ only on the multiplicative arm; $\beta_C$ has a separate behavioural interpretation as an action-prior shift, not as part of the inverse-temperature mapping.
- *RL hyperparameter.* Doya-NA-$\beta$ inverse temperature (Doya 2002 §3.3; Lee 2024 §3.4 Eq. 4 — $\beta^{-1}(s) = 1/\langle E(s,\hat a)\rangle_{\hat a}$).
- *Bayesian-brain precision level.* **Policy precision $\Pi_\pi$** — how sharply to commit to one policy. Under active inference (Friston, Rigoli et al. 2015), this is also the inverse-temperature on expected free energy $G(\pi)$ — but the active-inference and Doya readings diverge on what triggers a site-C response: under Doya, $|\delta|$ alone; under active inference, information-action selection under uncertainty (`professor-bayesian-brain` P2). v4 keeps the Doya reading as load-bearing; see §5.10.
- *Pain construct.* **Fear-avoidance as sustained tonic policy-entropy reduction** (`professor-pain-modeling` P-Pain-4 — Vlaeyen & Linton 2000; L'Haridon & Cañamero 2023 cortisol-modulated robot pain). Tonic site-C output is more negative (lower temperature, lower entropy) in spatial regions proximal to past injury events, *outlasting the injury input*. This contrast — near-injury vs far-from-injury action-distribution entropy in the same post-recovery phase — is the project-feasible signature.
- *Project-testbed measure.* (i) `delta_survival_clamp_C` largest on regime-change recovery (`professor-rl-bayesian-dl` P-3); (ii) `mod_h_to_beta_eff_R2` at C $\ge 0.5$ while same scalar at A, B < 0.3 (`professor-rl-bayesian-dl` P-1); (iii) per-tile visitation entropy in near-injury vs far-from-injury regions, post-recovery (`professor-pain-modeling` P-Pain-4); (iv) regression of $\gamma^{(C)}$ on value-prediction-error magnitude in per-site partial regression (`professor-bayesian-brain` P3).
- *Falsification signature.* If $\gamma_C$ effective rank is high (> 5) and the per-site clamp dominates all task axes, the site-C reading is overparameterised and the single-channel-NA-with-passengers reading wins (`professor-neuromodulation` P5).

### 5.4 Plasticity-gating predictions (site B, GRU update gate)

**Re-naming note (per math-reviewer flag (d)).** v3 named site B's hyperparameter as "effective discount on past memory" and gestured toward a 5-HT / γ_Bellman mapping with a $\tilde\gamma_{\text{Bellman}}$ caveat symbol. The math-reviewer audit ([film_neuromod_integration.md §10 flag (d)](../concepts/film_neuromod_integration.md); also [film_hypernet_bnn_lineage_and_critic_modulation.md §3.1](../concepts/film_hypernet_bnn_lineage_and_critic_modulation.md)) ruled this dimensionally incoherent: the GRU update gate $z_t \in (0,1)^H$ produces *per-unit hidden-state retention per inner-network-timestep*, with dimensional content bounded by $(0,1)$; the Bellman γ is a *global scalar* (typically ~0.99) governing the value-function horizon, with dimensional content "temporal weighting per environment-timestep". The two effects can correlate empirically (longer hidden-state memory tends to support longer-horizon credit assignment) but they are not the same quantity. v4 re-names site B's hyperparameter as **plasticity gating** — per-unit memory persistence — and drops the γ_Bellman framing entirely. The behavioural readout (memory protection across regime change) is preserved; only the named hyperparameter changes.

- *Cellular mechanism.* Additive arm dominant ($\beta_B$) — Ferguson & Cardin 2020 Box 1c (rheobase shift). The GRU update gate $z_t = \sigma(W_z [h_{t-1}, x_t] + b_z + \beta^{(B)})$ — additive $\beta^{(B)}$ shifts the "stay vs update" decision boundary without changing sensitivity to input, the rheobase analogue (`professor-neuromodulation` P3).
- *RL hyperparameter.* **Plasticity gating — per-unit hidden-state retention**, measurable as recurrent-state autocorrelation time-constant $\tau_h$. This is *not* a Bellman-γ modulation. Costacurta 2024 Prop. 1 (LSTM-equivalence of low-rank weight scaling) supplies the structural precedent — gated memory update is a learned-forget-gate. Lee 2024 §6 explicitly declined to commit to a 5-HT-discount mapping; v4 inherits Lee 2024's stance and names site B's role honestly.
- *Bayesian-brain precision level.* **State-transition precision $\Pi_z$** — how much to trust the prior over hidden states relative to incoming evidence. Adams, Shipp & Friston 2013.
- *Pain construct.* **Tonic prior-precision elevation** (`professor-pain-modeling` P-Pain-2 — Büchel et al. 2014; Wiech 2016; Tabor & Burr 2019). Post-recovery tonic $\gamma_A^{\mathcal{T}}$ stays elevated relative to pre-injury baseline without sustained actor-entropy change. This is the Bayesian-brain reading of chronic-pain-like hypervigilance: the threat prior's precision grows, not the policy temperature. The site-B modulation is what carries this elevation across the recovery window.
- *Project-testbed measure.* (i) `delta_survival_clamp_B` largest on memory-required predator-avoidance and long-dormancy R2/R3 return tasks (`professor-rl-bayesian-dl` P-3 + `professor-neuromodulation` P8); (ii) tonic-state ratio post-recovery / pre-injury baseline (`professor-pain-modeling` P-Pain-2); (iii) site-B response loading disproportionately on state-prediction-error magnitude in the per-site partial regression (`professor-bayesian-brain` P3); (iv) **recurrent-state autocorrelation time constant $\tau_h$** under modulated vs clamped site B (the corrected operationalisation per math-reviewer required revision 2 — the auto-correlation of the GRU hidden state, NOT a γ_Bellman estimate).
- *Falsification signature.* If site-B clamp's deficit is small or distributes uniformly across all subtests, the plasticity-gating reading is refuted and the site is a passenger head.

**Joint prediction across the three sites — the target-specificity test.** `professor-neuromodulation` P8 sharpens the per-site predictions into a *qualitative-profile* test: the three sites should produce *qualitatively dissociable* deficits, not just *quantitatively different* magnitudes. Operationally, the 3 × 3 deficit matrix `eval/per_site_freeze_per_subtest_drop` (A-freeze, B-freeze, C-freeze × noisy-obs, long-dormancy R2/R3 return, regime-change recovery) should show **diagonal dominance** — each site's freeze produces its largest drop on its predicted subtest. A flat matrix, or one with off-diagonal-dominant pattern, refutes the target-specificity claim and reduces the architectural contribution to a single-channel result.

### 5.5 Phasic-vs-tonic predictions (conditional on T/P split)

**Plain-English summary.** Biology dissociates two timescales of neuromodulator action: brief bursts (phasic, milliseconds–seconds) and sustained shifts (tonic, seconds–minutes). The corpus repeatedly invokes this dissociation but no published forward-pass FiLM-style machine has two recurrent timescales with separable readouts. The project's Phase 0 T/P split is the architectural change that makes the dissociation testable. The predictions in this subsection are *conditional on the split landing*; without it, predictions 5.5.1 and 5.5.2 are not identifiable.

- **§5.5.1 — Phasic-vs-tonic timescale separation (`professor-rl-bayesian-dl` P-7).** Post-T/P-split, the phasic state $P$ should track $|\delta|$ with an autocorrelation time-constant $\tau_P \in [1, 5]$ episodes; the tonic state $T$ should track cumulative cost or running value-baseline error with $\tau_T \in [50, 200]$ episodes. The separation factor $\tau_T / \tau_P \ge 10$ is the empirical signature of a successful split. WandB readouts: `mod_h_P_autocorr_tau`, `mod_h_T_autocorr_tau`. **Falsification**: $\tau_T / \tau_P < 3$ means the two recurrent compartments collapsed under optimisation pressure — the multi-channel claim remains non-identifiable on this testbed and v4's headline must be conditional on its success.
- **§5.5.2 — Differential phasic / tonic tracking content (`professor-neuromodulation` P2).** Phasic $P$ should track per-episode $|\delta|$ and lock to regime-change events; tonic $T$ should track a slow integral of cost / aversive-signal exposure and stay elevated long after a regime change passed. Two failure modes refute: (i) P and T converge (effective rank 1 across the two-state representation — the network found no use for two timescales); (ii) P tracks cumulative cost and T tracks $|\delta|$ (inverted assignment — refutes the Aston-Jones-derived attribution).
- **§5.5.3 — Tonic content under sustained aversive exposure (`professor-pain-modeling` P-Pain-2 + `professor-neuromodulation` P2 cross-link).** `professor-neuromodulation` left P2's tonic claim intentionally under-specified; `professor-pain-modeling` sharpens it into a *hysteresis claim*: the tonic state should enter a high near-absorbing state after sustained aversive exposure and not relax even when the aversive regime passes. This is the substrate-level signature of hypervigilance / chronic-pain analogues. **Construct-validity caveat (from `professor-pain-modeling` P-Pain-2)**: long $\tau_T$ alone is not a prior-precision claim. The dissociating feature requires a **cross-context transfer probe** — the modulator's persistent state generalises to a novel post-recovery context, not just to the same one. Without that probe (§5.8), this is "recurrent inertia mis-labelled as chronicity", not chronic-pain analog.

### 5.6 Architecture-discrimination predictions

These predictions discriminate the project's forward-pass FiLM substrate from architectural alternatives (hypernets, conditional batch norm, Rodriguez-Garcia 2026 gradient-level gain). They are the predictions that ride on substrate-specific signatures rather than on behaviour.

- **§5.6.1 — Effective rank of $\gamma$ at site C (`professor-rl-bayesian-dl` P-2; `professor-neuromodulation` P5).** SVD on $\gamma^{(C)}_t$ stacked across a regime-change window; effective rank $r_{\text{eff}}(\gamma^{(C)}) = (\sum_i \sigma_i)^2 / \sum_i \sigma_i^2$. **Prediction under the Doya-channel reading**: $r_{\text{eff}} \le 2.0$ (one scalar's worth of variation does the temperature work). **Prediction under the multi-channel-dissociation reading**: $r_{\text{eff}}^{(C)} > 1$ AND principal directions at A, B, C are orthogonal (cross-site principal angle large). WandB readouts: `gamma_C_effective_rank`, `gamma_AB_principal_angle`. **Vecoven 2020 Fig. 7 is the published precedent for this measurement.** Costacurta 2024 Fig. 3F is the closest dissociation-by-ablation precedent. Falsification: $r_{\text{eff}}^{(C)} \ge 5$ → FiLM at C is doing something Doya doesn't describe, and the v4 claim should be "multi-dimensional gain controller", not "RL-hyperparameter modulator".
- **§5.6.2 — Cross-site cross-correlation lag structure (`professor-neuromodulation` P6).** Pearson cross-correlation between $\gamma^{(A)}(t), \gamma^{(B)}(t), \gamma^{(C)}(t)$ at lags $\in \{-5, -1, 0, 1, 5\}$ episodes. **Pre-T/P prediction**: all zero-lag cross-correlations > 0.9 — confirms single-channel-with-passengers. **Post-T/P prediction**: zero-lag cross-correlations drop to < 0.7 and lag-structure emerges with $\gamma^{(B)}$ trailing $\gamma^{(C)}$ by several episodes. Post-T/P zero-lag cross-correlations remaining > 0.9 refute the dissociation — either the split was insufficient or the task does not demand multi-channel work.
- **§5.6.3 — Hessian-eigenvalue probe discriminating forward-pass FiLM from Rodriguez-Garcia 2026 (`professor-rl-bayesian-dl` P-6; corrected per math-reviewer required revision 1).** Around each regime change, compute top-$k$ eigenvalues of the PPO loss Hessian over a small batch (`jax.hessian` or Hessian-vector products via `jax.jvp(jax.grad(L))`). **Corrected discriminator direction** (per math-reviewer audit of concept memo §6.3 signature 2): under Rodriguez-Garcia 2026, the *raw-weight Hessian* $H_W$ is **amplified by $g^2$** (not flattened); the flattening lives at the effective-parameter-space Hessian $H_{W_{\text{eff}}} = H_W / g^2$. The discriminating measurement is therefore the **shift direction of $H_W$** — amplification under Rodriguez-Garcia, no change under forward-pass FiLM. **Prediction**: FiLM agent and unmodulated baseline both show *similar* top-eigenvalue trajectories peri-switch at $H_W$ (no amplification); a Rodriguez-Garcia-style NGM-SGD agent if implemented as a comparator should show $H_W$ amplification proportional to $g^2$. **Falsification (Failure mode 1)**: FiLM agent shows $H_W$ amplification — the orthogonality claim in §3 is wrong; forward-pass FiLM is leaking into optimiser dynamics (publishable finding in its own right). **Failure mode 2**: unmodulated baseline shows amplification (PPO's clipping providing it via a different route) — the discriminating measure is not load-bearing.
- **§5.6.4 — Per-feature gain variance discriminating FiLM from hypernet (`professor-rl-bayesian-dl` P-9).** Compute $\text{Var}_t[\gamma_i^{(C)}]$ across a regime-change window for each feature index $i$; report the distribution of per-feature variances. **Prediction**: FiLM shows high p90/p10 ratio (a few features carry the change); a hypothetical hypernet ablation should show lower ratio (change distributed across more features). Falsification: uniform per-feature variance → FiLM is collapsing to a Rodriguez-Garcia-style scalar gain and the high-dimensional FiLM substrate is wasted. This is "go simpler — drop FiLM in favour of a 1-scalar gain".
- **§5.6.5 — Cross-seed channel-attribution consistency (`professor-rl-bayesian-dl` P-10).** Train two identical FiLM agents with different random seeds *and* different random initialisations of the three read-out matrices. Check whether the per-site clamp-deficit profile is consistent (Spearman correlation across seed pairs ≥ 0.5). High consistency: the task breaks the symmetry and the Doya-channel attribution is recoverable. Low consistency (< 0.3): channel attribution is seed-dependent — site A is NA in some runs and ACh in others — and the strong-form claim "A is the ACh site" must be walked back to "ACh-α-style behaviour emerges at one of the three sites, with the assignment being seed-specific". This is the *identifiability prerequisite test* the single-`mod_h` design imposes; it should be run on the final v4 checkpoints regardless of which outcome carries §5.1.

### 5.7 Pain-construct validity gate-keeping

This subsection is the most important contribution of `professor-pain-modeling` to v3 / v4. It declares, in compact form, what dissociations the project must satisfy *before* claiming its results are pain-relevant rather than "generic RL modulation on a pain-flavoured testbed", **and** declares what behavioural patterns do *not* count even if they look pain-relevant. It exists so that, when v4's results land, the project can pre-commit to the strict-construct claim and survive a *PAIN* / *Nature Mental Health* reviewer, or retreat to the sober "interoceptive neuromodulation as a substrate for pain-like behaviour" formulation without rhetorical embarrassment.

**The four-dissociation gate (current-testbed-feasible under the T/P split prerequisite).**

1. **Channel-selective $\gamma$, not volume control** (P-Pain-1 — operationalised at site A in §5.2). Without this, the modulator is a 1-D Doya-NA temperature signal that broadcasts uniformly; the "pain" framing retreats to "interoceptive neuromodulation produces hypervigilance-like survival benefits" — L'Haridon & Cañamero 2023 at higher fidelity, not a pain-computation claim.
2. **Time-locked to the interoceptive injury signal, not the threat input** (P-Pain-1 caveat — gain shifts lag injury onset by $\le \min(5, 2\tau_{\text{mod}})$ steps regardless of predator-presence change). Dissociates pain-computation from task-relevance-learning.
3. **Within-trial cross-domain coupling, $r \ge 0.3$ at lag $\le 5$** (the joint hypervigilance fingerprint from `pain_vs_nociception_construct.md` §2.2 — site-A gain shift, site-B memory shift, and site-C policy shift co-vary on the same trial, same seed). Dissociates "pain syndrome" (Vlaeyen-Linton joint perceptual-mnemonic-policy reweighting through a common modulator) from independent regulation. Under single-`mod_h` this is by construction; under the T/P split it becomes testable on dissociable channels.
4. **Dissociation from the C1 EMA baseline** (`pain_vs_nociception_construct.md` §3). The modulated agent's signature must differ from a trainable exponential-moving-average-of-nociception baseline wired through the same A/B/C heads. The contrast metric $\Delta_t \gamma_{\mathcal{T}}^{(i)} - \Delta_t \gamma_{\mathcal{T}}^{(j)}$ between two threat channels with different injury-scaled noise profiles is the pre-registrable dissociator. Without this, "pain computation" collapses to "pain smoothing".

**Behavioural signatures that do NOT count (P-Pain-5 negative predictions).**

- **(P-Pain-5a)** Survival increase under modulator on regime change. *Consistent with* the v2 unification at the RL level, *consistent with* continual-learning consolidation, *consistent with* NA-as-context-detector. Not evidence on any of the four dissociations above. The R2 empirical anchor is *motivating*, not evidentiary on construct content.
- **(P-Pain-5b)** Modulator burst at injury onset. The canonical NA / unexpected-uncertainty signature (Wainstein 2025; Aston-Jones & Cohen 2005). Not pain-content on its own.
- **(P-Pain-5c)** Slow recovery of survival after injury. Consistent with any slow-state architecture (any recurrent module with long time constant). Requires transfer across rungs without per-rung surgery (§5.8 testbed-adequacy boundary).
- **(P-Pain-5d)** Increased aversion to nociceptive input. This is what reward shaping on `extero_noc` produces. Pain content requires dissociability from the input — the posterior moves when the prior moves without the input changing (the placebo direction; future work).
- **(P-Pain-5e)** Continual-learning resistance. This *is* the v2 redirect — continual-learning improvement is *one readout* of state-dependent hyperparameter modulation, not pain content.

**Construct-validity verdict.** If all four dissociations hold, "pain-like hypervigilance signature" is defensible at a *PAIN* or *Nature Mental Health* reviewer. If any one fails, v4 retreats to the formulation "**interoceptive neuromodulation as a substrate for pain-like behaviour**" (`pain_vs_nociception_construct.md` §4.4). The R2 empirical anchor is — *by itself* — not evidence on any of the four dissociations; it is consistent with all four and consistent with all four being false.

### 5.8 Testbed-adequacy boundary

This subsection declares, prediction-by-prediction, what the current testbed plus the T/P split tests; what a small probe-trial extension would add; and what a larger placebo / expectation extension would need. The intent is that v4 commits only to what is testable on the current substrate, and pre-flags the extensions that would lift specific predictions out of the "future work" bucket.

**What the current testbed + T/P split tests directly:**

- §5.1 — combined γ/β-clamp + |δ|-tracking falsifier at site C, except the $d'$/criterion refinement (which needs a forced-choice probe).
- §5.2 / §5.3 / §5.4 — per-site (A/C/B) clamp deficits, target-specificity matrix, per-modality $\gamma_A$ statistics, per-tile entropy in near-injury vs far-from-injury regions, recurrent-state autocorrelation $\tau_h$ under modulated vs clamped site B.
- §5.5.1 — phasic / tonic timescale separation, autocorrelation time-constants.
- §5.5.2 — differential phasic / tonic tracking content (P vs |δ|; T vs cumulative cost).
- §5.6 — all five architecture-discrimination predictions (effective rank, cross-site cross-correlation, Hessian-eigenvalue with corrected $H_W$-amplification direction, per-feature gain variance, cross-seed channel-attribution consistency).
- §5.7 four-dissociation gate — three of four are current-testbed-feasible (channel-selective $\gamma$; time-locked to injury signal; within-trial cross-domain coupling). The fourth (C1 EMA baseline contrast) requires the C1 baseline configuration to be implemented; this is a senior-developer item but does not require new task design.

**What a small probe-trial extension adds (`professor-pain-modeling` P-Pain-2 + §5.5.3; `professor-bayesian-brain` P4):**

- Post-recovery predator-cue probe trial with no injury risk — enables the cross-context transfer test for §5.5.3 (tonic hysteresis as chronic-pain-analog vs recurrent inertia mis-labelled).
- Forced-choice discrimination probe at fixed grid positions — enables the $d'$/criterion refinement of §5.1 Outcome U and the precision-vs-temperature dissociation at site C (`professor-bayesian-brain` P4).

These are small senior-developer / experiment-designer additions and do not require architecture changes beyond the T/P split.

**What a larger extension would need (flagged as future work, not committed in v4):**

- **Information-action ("look") probe** (`professor-bayesian-brain` P2) — a costly, no-movement action that returns a denoised observation for $k$ subsequent steps. Required for the EFE / epistemic-value reading of site C (active inference vs Doya-NA). Non-trivial: adds a new action class and an observation-corruption schedule.
- **Cue × injury contingency learning** (`professor-pain-modeling` P-Pain-6 placebo analog) — learned cue predicts injury in pre-training, presented at test without an injury actually occurring. Required for the placebo / nocebo analog: prior-shift produces posterior-shift without input change. Non-trivial: adds a new associative-learning sub-task.
- **Immediate-vs-delayed-reward variant + learned-discount head** — required for any γ_Bellman / 5-HT channel test. Out-of-substrate for v4 (see §5.9); a v5 follow-up that adopts Option B from the [investigation memo §3.5](../concepts/film_hypernet_bnn_lineage_and_critic_modulation.md) would need this.

**Predictions that the current testbed cannot identify:**

- The 5-HT / discount-factor branch (Doya 2002 §3.2) is not testable on the forward-pass FiLM substrate at all — see §5.9. The grid-world does not natively dissociate immediate from delayed reward either, so even with a hybrid extension the gradient signal on a learned-discount head would be weak.
- The forward-pass-only vs gradient-level disambiguation (§5.6.3) is testable on the FiLM side but does not have a Rodriguez-Garcia-style NGM-SGD comparator without that comparator being implemented. v4 commits only to the FiLM-side measurement; the comparator is a future-work item.

### 5.9 What the substrate explicitly does NOT carry

**Plain-English summary.** v4's unification claim is anchored on two channels (α at site A, β at site C) and one re-named third (plasticity gating at site B) — *not* four. This subsection draws the honesty boundary at the predictions level, matching the concept memo's §5.5 no-map boundary at the operator level. Three classes of modulation that look like they should belong to this substrate but do not.

**No-map 1 — γ_Bellman / 5-HT (Doya 2002 §3.2).** The 5-HT-as-discount mapping has *no implementation* in either the FiLM corpus or the neuromodulation corpus. Lee 2024 §6 explicitly dropped 5-HT in their two-channel Doya-DaYu agent because "no convergent normative theory exists"; γ stays fixed at 0.95 in their bandit experiments. Xing 2022, Wang 2024, and Rodriguez-Garcia 2026 all leave γ_Bellman fixed. The mathematical reason ([investigation memo §3](../concepts/film_hypernet_bnn_lineage_and_critic_modulation.md)) is that γ_Bellman is a *scalar inside a temporal sum* governing the value-function horizon, whereas a forward-pass FiLM operator produces *per-feature affine on activations*. The clean architectural home for γ_Bellman is the TD-target computation $y_t = r_t + \gamma(c) V(s_{t+1})$ — at the loss / learning-update layer, *not* the forward pass. A FiLM substrate cannot carry this channel without architectural extension into a hybrid system (forward-pass FiLM + a learned-discount head feeding the TD target, [investigation memo §3.2 Candidate d](../concepts/film_hypernet_bnn_lineage_and_critic_modulation.md)). **v4 follows Lee 2024's lead and acknowledges this boundary**: the four-channel Doya mapping is restricted to two cleanly carried channels (α, β) plus one structurally distinct re-named site (plasticity gating), with γ_Bellman / 5-HT named as out-of-substrate. **Future work**: the hybrid architecture from investigation-memo Option B is the v5 route if the project later wants the γ channel. v4 does not commit to this.

**No-map 2 — gradient-level gain (Rodriguez-Garcia 2026).** Rodriguez-Garcia 2026's neuromodulator-gated SGD (NGM-SGD) modulates the *gradient* via $W_{ij}(t) = g_i(t) w_{ij}(t)$, reparameterising the learning rate as $\lambda \to \lambda / g^2$. This is complementary to the forward-pass FiLM substrate — both invoke noradrenergic gain at orthogonal points (forward activation vs gradient). The two could combine, but no paper has done so. The §5.6.3 Hessian-eigenvalue probe is the project-side measurement that would discriminate the two substrates if both were instantiated; v4 commits only to the FiLM side. Rodriguez-Garcia is the structural analogue of what an Option-B v5 extension would look like — both keep the forward-pass FiLM in place and bolt a second mechanism (gradient-level for NA-α; learned-discount-head for 5-HT-γ) onto the loss / gradient.

**No-map 3 — internal-RNN gain (Wainstein 2025).** Wainstein's $g$ is an experimenter-varied dial on a *post-hoc* trained-RNN analysis, not a side-signal-conditioned forward-pass operator. The "side signal" (pupil diameter) correlates with $g$ but is not the *generator* of $g$ — the RNN was trained without pupil input. Different mechanism entirely; the integration paper's position (already stated in [film_neuromod_integration.md §5.5](../concepts/film_neuromod_integration.md)) is that Wainstein is the corpus's strongest *empirical anchor* for the cellular-gain ↔ behavioural-switch link, not a FiLM-variant. Carried into v4 unchanged.

### 5.10 Future work flagged but not committed in v4

Two items are flagged here so that v4's predictions section is closed on the current substrate, but the project has a record of where v5 (or a parallel sub-project) could go:

1. **Active-inference / EFE temperature reading at site C** (`professor-bayesian-brain` P2 + P7). The Friston / Pezzulo / Parr active-inference literature is out-of-corpus; the EFE-temperature reading is mathematically rich but its discriminating test (information-seeking under high observation noise) requires the information-action probe (§5.8 above). v4 keeps the Doya-NA reading as the load-bearing claim at C and flags the EFE reading as a future-work alternative. The current $d'$/criterion refinement (§5.1 + P4 above) is the down-payment — it gives v5 the data needed to revisit the choice without committing v4.
2. **γ_Bellman / 5-HT as a hybrid extension** ([investigation memo §3.5 Option B](../concepts/film_hypernet_bnn_lineage_and_critic_modulation.md)). v4 adopts Option A — drop the 5-HT/γ channel from substrate claims. A v5 could adopt Option B — add a learned-discount network $\hat\gamma(c)$ as a fourth modulator arm consumed in the TD target $y_t = r_t + \hat\gamma(c) V(s_{t+1})$. This breaks the substrate's FiLM-uniformity (one arm wouldn't be FiLM) and requires an immediate-vs-delayed-reward grid-world variant to give the learned-discount head a useful gradient signal. v4 keeps the substrate uniform; v5 is the place to revisit.
