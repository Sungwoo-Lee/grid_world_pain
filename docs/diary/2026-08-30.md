### Session `4efbe660` — Sensor-ladder analysis: 15 figures, 3 corrections after review

**Full session UUID** (for `claude --resume`): `4efbe660-28c2-4643-b231-d3c6d2635b5a`

**What this session did** (in plain words):

- Collected 300,000 evaluation episodes for each of the 14 `lad_*` sensor arms (~996M step rows) and built a run-agnostic analysis folder: one shared sweep plus one self-contained script per figure.
- Two reviewers ran against the finished verdict: a fresh-reader pass (jargon, figure legibility, misleading scales) and an adversarial analysis review.
- Three real errors were found and fixed. A confounded reference pairing inflated the cost of a shorter visual range fourfold (-33.5 -> -7.9 steps); the whole-episode injury result was explained by a mechanism that predicts the wrong sign; and "13 of 14 arms agree" was written as if the arms were independent trials when they share a seed and bit-identical worlds.
- Chasing the second of those produced the best result in the study: a new step-by-step sweep showing the assigned wound is half healed by step 17 and 90% gone by step 28, the extra hiding peaks at +7.5 to +13.5 pp around step 14-16 and fades with it, and the agent then hides LESS while repaying a 14-20 point food debt.
- Published the artifact and recorded 17 new entries in the artifact-generation guide.

**Headline finding**:

Identity-resolving sight, not acuity, is what stops the agent hiding from harmless rabbits (9 arms vs 5, four measures, no overlap) - and a randomised wound causes a transient burst of hiding that tracks the wound's own healing curve and is then repaid in foraging.

**What's next**:

1. Replicate the ladder at 2-3 seeds. This is the single change that moves the study from exploratory to confirmatory: all 14 arms share seed 42, so no effect size here is separable from run-to-run variation.
2. Act on the review's open items: the predator-odour control is contaminated by the intensity/discriminability anticorrelation, and the identity-vs-capacity confound in Q1/Q2 needs an arm that separates them.
3. Consider a world-level bootstrap over the shared 300k episodes to put an interval on the arm-level correlations.
