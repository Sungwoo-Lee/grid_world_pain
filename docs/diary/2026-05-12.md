The example you gave was the load-bearing one: when an agent is feeding and a predator approaches, does it actually stop and hide in a bush? **Headline finding** (refines, does not contradict, the prior R2.5 verdict): under matched olfactory smells, the agent IS class-discriminating — but at the EVENT level, not the spatial-trajectory level. The numbers:

- Bush-dive rate: **+37 percentage points higher near a predator** (87.6%) than near a rabbit (50.8%) in Cell C.

The toolkit is reusable — every future hypervigilance / NMN / dreamer study can switch it on without further engineering.
