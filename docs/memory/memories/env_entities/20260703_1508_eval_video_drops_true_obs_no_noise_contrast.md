# Training video-eval drops true_obs -> noise is invisible in the sensory panel

## Key conclusion
The video renderer's sensory panel shows perceptual noise as the DIFFERENCE between the noisy `obs` and the clean `true_obs`. But the training-time video eval never saves `true_obs`: `evaluation_core.py` ~L183 gates `record_true_obs = config.record_true_observations AND record_stats`, and train.py runs eval in two passes — Pass 1 VIDEO (render_video=True, record_stats=False) and Pass 2 STATS (record_stats=True). Because the video pass has record_stats=False, the `.rec.gz` stores `true_obs=None`. `build_sensory_viz` (sensor.py) then does `true_obs = obs`, so both sub-panels are identical and the noise looks absent. Fix (developer, written, left uncommitted): record true_obs in the video path too when `params.perceptual_noise_enabled`. Immediate workaround: `scripts/eval/eval_rollout.py --record` already records `true_obs` (apply_noise=False), so a manually-generated video shows the contrast.

## Evidence, measurements, facts
- basic/07 training recording (models/500003 era): obs (139,27) present, `true_obs: None`. eval_rollout-generated recording: true_obs present, |obs-true_obs| olfaction-block sum ~67 (noise visible).
- Affects EVERY perceptual-noise config's training-eval video, not just level 07.
