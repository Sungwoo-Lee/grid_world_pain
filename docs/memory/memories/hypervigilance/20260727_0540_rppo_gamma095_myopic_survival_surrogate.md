# rPPO baselines (gamma=0.95) optimize a myopic surrogate in which death is nearly invisible

## Key conclusion

The task's controllable signal is the rare terminal death penalty ([[20260727_0539_task_different_in_kind_reward_algebra]]), and the discount factor decides whether an agent can SEE that signal from a distance. All live rPPO baselines train at gamma=0.95 — verified from the SAVED config of a live run (results/JAX_RecurrentPPO/20260726-051209_rppo_b03_mc_dp1_n110/models/config.yaml:367), independently re-checked by the PI, not from source YAML. At 0.95 a death 40 steps in the future contributes 0.95^40·100 ≈ 1.3% of its magnitude — rPPO effectively optimizes a ~20-step horizon in which survival (our canonical metric) barely appears. Dreamer trains at gamma=0.997 (horizon 333). Consequences: (1) every rPPO-vs-Dreamer survival comparison to date compares agents optimizing DIFFERENT objectives; (2) the rPPO plateau at ~177 survival steps after 15.8M episodes is weak evidence about task difficulty and possibly strong evidence of objective mis-specification; (3) the rPPO baselines feeding both papers may need re-running at a survival-appropriate gamma before any cross-algorithm claim is printed.

## Evidence, measurements, facts

- 0.95^40 ≈ 0.129 (death at 40 steps ≈ 0.13/100 of value); 0.95^14 ≈ 0.49 (half-life ~14 steps).
- rPPO plateau: 161.7→177.2 steps and 0.42→0.34 hits/100st across 2.4M→15.8M episodes (6.7x data for ~10%).
- Note: gamma varies per agent config — recurrent_ppo_M.yaml carries 0.95; VERIFY each run's saved models/config.yaml before reuse (some configs may differ).
