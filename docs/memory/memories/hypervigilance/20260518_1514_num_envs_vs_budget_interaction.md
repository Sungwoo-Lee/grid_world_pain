# Size-vs-num_envs winner depends on the training budget

## Key conclusion
On the 10×10 hypervigilance task with dreamer-srl v2, the optimal **(size, num_envs)** pair depends on the training-step budget, not just the task. At short budgets (200k env steps, Phase 1 sweep), low parallelism (num_envs=4) won and the size winner was S. At long budgets (≥ 2M, Long-Budget + Extended sweep), high parallelism (num_envs=16-64) catches up dramatically — XS/16/2M reaches 154 (vs XS/16/200k=73, +110%) and XS/64/2M reaches 139 (vs XS/64/200k=44, +216%) — and the size winner flips to XS. The mechanism is that high-parallelism configs have more world-model warm-up to amortise, so they look worse at short budget but eventually pull ahead because of larger replay diversity. Operational lesson: **short-budget hyperparameter sweeps systematically under-rank high-parallelism cells, so a short-budget winner is not a reliable indicator of the long-budget production recipe.** Run sweep cells at the budget you actually plan to deploy at, or scale-up the leading short-budget candidates explicitly.

## Evidence, measurements, facts
- Short-budget sweep (Phase 1, 200k steps each cell): envs=4 wins; size winner = S.
- Long-budget sweep (LB + Extended, 2M-4M steps each cell): envs=16-64 wins; size winner = XS.
- Specific cell deltas (Q5 ep_len_avg):
  - XS / envs=16 / 200k = 73 → XS / envs=16 / 2M = 154 (**+110%**).
  - XS / envs=64 / 200k = 44 → XS / envs=64 / 2M = 139 (**+216%**).
  - S / envs=4 / 200k won Phase 1 sweep, but at 2M is beaten by XS / envs=16-64.
- Sheeprl baseline anchor: ep_len_avg ≈ 106 on the same env.
- The pattern matches a "warm-up amortisation" story: high-num_envs runs spend a larger fraction of early steps filling replay with random data, so the per-step learning curve looks shallow at short budget but the asymptote is higher.
- Consistent with the broader DreamerV3 literature pattern that learning-starts cost is amortised by total budget, and the size-vs-parallelism trade-off shifts as the budget grows.

## Decisions and actions
- **Sweep-design rule promoted to memory**: do not pick a production recipe from a short-budget hyperparameter sweep without re-running the top 2-3 candidates at the deployment budget. The short-budget ranking can flip.
- The 10×10 hypervigilance production recipe ([[20260518_1513_production_recipe_xs_16_4m_hypervigilance]]) was chosen from the long-budget sweep specifically because of this finding; it would not have been chosen from Phase 1.
- The M cell adds a second wrinkle on top of this — see [[20260518_1515_m_paradox_resolution_slow_learner]]: size-vs-budget is also regime-dependent, and M cells may overtake XS at budgets beyond 4M.

## Open questions and follow-ups
- Is the +110%/+216% gap a generic dreamer-srl pattern or specific to this env? Worth a small ablation on the 5×5 food-only env at envs={4, 16, 64} × budget={200k, 2M} before generalising.
- The size flip (S at short budget → XS at long budget) was specific to num_envs choice; we did not run a full size sweep at envs=4 at long budget. Open: would S at envs=4 / 4M beat XS at envs=16 / 4M? Not in this sweep.
