# Statistical significance is "cheap" on a near-deterministic probe

## Key conclusion
The avoidance probe with small initial-state jitter has tiny within-model variance, so Welch's t → ∞ and Cohen's d → ∞ for ANY non-zero mean gap (from `t ≈ Δ / (s·√(1/n1+1/n2))` as s→0). p-values and even Cohen's d are therefore inflated and near-meaningless for model comparisons. Decide with **absolute magnitude in real units + distribution overlap against a reference scale**, using p only as a sanity gate. Concretely: **spatial spread (radius of gyration) is statistically significant** between the mature and early models (Welch/Mann–Whitney p≪Bonferroni, paired p=0.002, Cohen's d 1.5–2.3) **but a WEAK discriminator** — both models roam ~4–5 cells (a ~0.6-cell gap), whereas bush-use separates them categorically (100% vs 13% entered-bush, no overlap).

## Evidence, measurements, facts
- Test set: mature `randinit` (8.9M) vs early `randpred` (1.5M), radius of gyration, 30 seeds each, per condition. Non-parametric (Mann–Whitney) and parametric (Welch) agreed; a condition-level paired Wilcoxon/paired-t gave p=0.002 (randpred systematically higher).
- Inflation example: no-animal · inj0, R_g 3.19±0.03 vs 3.67±0.11 → Cohen's d ≈ 5.7, p ~1e-21 — an absurd "effect" purely because std is ±0.03.
- Companion stat concepts used (all in the tutorial): Welch's t (unequal-variance means), Mann–Whitney U (rank-based, no normality), Cohen's d + pooled SD (effect size, n-independent), paired t + Wilcoxon signed-rank (systematic shift across conditions), Bonferroni α/k (family-wise error over 12 tests).
- A rendered-math tutorial was written: `docs/experiments/active/behavior_measures/model_comparison_statistics_tutorial.md` (linked from the study anchor). Radius of gyration = √(var(row)+var(col)) (RMS from the path centroid) — preferred over raw variance (cells², uninterpretable) and over mean-distance (RMS is the standard, more sensitive to wide roaming).

## Decisions and actions
- Keep spatial spread as a graded roaming measure but do NOT lean on it as a primary run-vs-hide discriminator (bush-use carries that).
- Report effect size + absolute gap alongside every p; treat near-deterministic-probe p-values with suspicion.
