# Foraging: hunger sets the timing, and the opening move is a fixed prior

## Key conclusion
In the controlled foraging probe (single food a fixed distance away, no threat, injury 0), **starting nutrition changes the *timing* of foraging, not the path**. The hungrier the agent, the sooner it sets off; the fuller it dithers (Rests) longer before bothering to eat. The clean satiety measure is **pre-departure delay**. Separately, the agent's reproducible "step up first, then go" is a **fixed opening-action prior**, not an adaptive gradient probe — but after that fixed opening it follows the (non-directional) smell gradient correctly to food in *all four* directions.

## Evidence, measurements, facts
- Probe deterministic across 8 seeds (±0); 1 episode/level suffices until randomness/threat is added.
- Departure delay rises with satiety: nutr 20/25 → leaves immediately (~1 step); 50/75 → ~3; 100 → ~5.
- Reach-food latency is U-shaped: nutr25 fastest (9 steps), nutr100 slow (dithering, 14), nutr20 slow (13) for a different reason — a near-floor **"desperation meander"** (leaves at once but wanders before committing).
- **Survival/death floor**: with food 6 cells away, nutrition ≤15 starves en route (0/8 survive), ≥20 survives (8/8). Floor moves with food distance.
- Direction test (food at grid centre, agent on each of 4 sides): in ALL runs the **first action is "Up"** regardless of food bearing → fixed opening prior. Efficiency depends on alignment: food-up (bottom start) near-optimal; food-down (top start) wastes steps (opening walks into the wall). Foraging capability is preserved in every direction.
- Olfactory sensor reports intensity only (≈1/dist^p, decay_power 2.0), no direction — so directional approach is genuine gradient-following.

## Decisions and actions
- Promoted `forage_nutrition` and `forage_direction` probe sets to `core/`.
- Candidate measures: pre-departure delay (satiety), path-inefficiency (near-floor desperation), first-action prior.

## Open questions and follow-ups
- Why always "Up"? Hypothesis: the fresh recurrent state at episode reset (no obs history) falls back to a learned default action. Test via first-action distribution from many random starts, or warm-up steps.
- Phase 1b naturalistic (random food placement) to confirm the signal survives outside fixed geometry.
