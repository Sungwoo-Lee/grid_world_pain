# M-size cells learn slowly, not weakly — the M paradox resolved

## Key conclusion
Mid-sweep observation: M-size cells (sheeprl preset 640/1024/mlp_layers=3) of dreamer-srl v2 had the **lowest** world-model loss across all extended-sweep cells, yet their ep_len_avg appeared stuck at low values (~50) — an apparent paradox where the WM is more accurate but the policy is worse. Resolution by late-iteration observation: M cells DO learn, just later than XS or S. E7 (M / num_envs=64 / 2M steps) went from a stuck Q3=50 to **Q4=59 → Q5=83** (rapid late climb) at 82% of total budget. The reading is that M's higher capacity needs proportionally more warm-up before its policy head catches up to the world-model accuracy; XS converges fast on a worse asymptote, M converges slow on a higher asymptote. **Capacity is not the bottleneck; warm-up time is.** This rules out the initial worry that M was simply over-parameterised for this env and locked in a bad regime — that hypothesis is refuted.

## Evidence, measurements, facts
- E7 trajectory (M / 64 / 2M, ep_len_avg by quintile): Q1≈45, Q2≈47, Q3=50, Q4=59, **Q5=83** (still climbing at run end).
- E8 (M / 128 / 2M) at 57% completion: Q3=58 (climbing pattern consistent with E7).
- WM loss ranking: M < S < XS across the sweep (M is the most accurate world-model, as expected from capacity).
- Compare XS / 16 / 4M = 184 (the production winner — see [[20260518_1513_production_recipe_xs_16_4m_hypervigilance]]): XS converges fast on 184; M is still at 83 at 2M, with steep upward slope.
- Hypothesis from the WM-loss-vs-policy-perf gap: the actor's REINFORCE estimator needs many imagined roll-outs through a well-tuned world model before the policy gradient sharpens; M's larger model means more replay needed to populate that quality.
- Refuted alternatives:
  - "M is over-parameterised for the env" → no, WM loss is the lowest.
  - "M is in a bad optimum" → no, late-iteration climb shows the optimisation is making consistent progress, not stuck.
  - "M's actor head is mis-sized vs the WM" → not investigated this session; cannot rule out, but the late breakout argues against it.

## Decisions and actions
- The production recipe stays XS at the 4M budget (see [[20260518_1513_production_recipe_xs_16_4m_hypervigilance]]) but with the explicit caveat that M may overtake XS at budgets ≥ 4M. The §10.4 second-pass synthesis (pending E7+E8 completion) should re-evaluate.
- Open a follow-up: run M / 64 / 4M (or 8M) to see if M's late climb crosses the XS asymptote.
- Generalisable rule for future-Claude on this codebase: **"M-size dreamer-srl cells need more iterations than XS; do not conclude 'M is not learning' from a single short run. Check the quintile-by-quintile ep_len trajectory; a flat early curve + climbing late curve = slow learner, not stuck."**
